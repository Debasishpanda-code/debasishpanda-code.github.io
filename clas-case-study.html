CLAS — Class Link Automation System

A training-operations platform designed and built independently to reduce manual coordination around recurring online classes. CLAS brings timetable processing, Zoom meeting operations, trainer communication, meeting records, recording administration and execution history into one workflow-oriented application.

01 / OPERATIONAL CHALLENGE

Recurring classes create repeatable coordination work.

Training operations require teams to turn timetable data into online meeting links, distribute those links to the right trainers and learners, avoid creating duplicate meetings, maintain a record of sessions and monitor what happened during each run. Separate spreadsheets, platform screens and manual follow-ups make it harder to see what is complete and where human intervention is required.

02 / PLATFORM DESIGN

One operational workspace across the class lifecycle.

Timetable-to-meeting workflow

Upload an Excel timetable or class list, run a validation/test path, then create real Zoom meetings for trainers to use.

Meeting records

Central list of created meetings with class, trainer, schedule, session and status context. The interface describes the shared record as the mechanism that prevents a class receiving a second link.

Review and exception handling

Review unresolved rows from a previous run, settle matching questions and retain run history for traceability.

Recording administration

Search recording inventory, inspect retention windows and manage cleanup with recoverability information shown in the interface.

Trainer and resource management

Maintain trainer, faculty, campus and account records used by the operational workflows.

Operational dashboard

Monitor daily classes, pending links, meeting counts, cleanup activity, grading queue indicators and recent system activity.
